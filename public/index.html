    Exclusive institutional community

    Only students of Institutions of National Importance

    Eligibility follows the official legal designation: institutions declared Institutions of National Importance by an Act of Parliament and listed by the Ministry of Education. Ranking or reputation alone doesn’t qualify an institution.

    • Students verify with their institutional email or a student ID reviewed by our team.
    • Every institution record cites its governing Act and an official source.
    • Recruiters see a community where every profile is real.
    About HORIZON

    A trusted bridge between India’s top institutions and the world

    HORIZON connects students of India’s Institutions of National Importance with international internships at companies, research institutes, think tanks, NGOs and government-backed programmes. We verify the people on both sides, review every listing, and make the whole process — from application to grievance — visible to the people it affects.
