Terms of use

The rules that keep HORIZON trustworthy for students, recruiters and institutions. Last updated 18 November 2026.

This is a demonstration deployment. These terms describe how the platform is designed to operate; have them reviewed by a qualified lawyer before any real launch.

1. Who can use HORIZON

Students must be currently enrolled at an institution declared an Institution of National Importance under an Act of Parliament and listed by the Ministry of Education. You verify your enrolment through your institutional email address or by submitting a student ID for manual review. Selecting an institution from a list is never enough on its own.

Recruiters must be authorised to hire on behalf of a genuine organisation. You verify through a work email at the organisation’s domain and supporting documents reviewed by HORIZON.

2. Accurate information

You agree to give accurate information about yourself, your studies or your organisation. CVs must belong to the account holder. HORIZON runs automated checks on CVs and may ask for changes, reject documents, or suspend accounts that provide false information.

3. No fees — ever

HORIZON never charges students to register, apply or be interviewed. Recruiters must never ask candidates for any payment (registration, training, security deposit, equipment or similar). Listings that ask for fees are removed and the recruiter’s account may be suspended. Report any fee request through the grievance desk.

4. Listings and applications

Every listing is reviewed before it is published and shows its source and last-verified date. For listings marked “Apply on HORIZON”, your validated CV, optional video resume and profile are shared with that organisation when you apply. For other listings you apply through the employer’s own process; HORIZON helps you track it but does not submit applications for you.

Listings labelled “Illustrative listing” are demonstration data and are not real vacancies.

5. Recruiter obligations

  • Respond to every HORIZON application within 14 days and give rejected candidates a short reason or next step.
  • Keep listings accurate, including compensation, visa support and dates.
  • Use candidate information only for the recruitment it was shared for.
  • Respond to grievances when the grievance desk requests it.

6. Conduct

Harassment, discrimination, impersonation, scraping, sharing others’ personal data and attempts to bypass verification or security controls are prohibited.

7. Grievances

Anyone can raise a grievance at /grievance-redressal. We acknowledge grievances within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days (72 hours for urgent safety issues). You can escalate to the Grievance Officer and reopen a decision within the published windows.

8. Visa and immigration information

Destination guides contain general information with links to official government sources. They are not legal or immigration advice; always confirm requirements with the official source and your employer.

9. Suspension and termination

We may suspend accounts that breach these terms. You will be told the reason and can contest the decision through the grievance desk.
