Privacy policy

What we collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. Last updated 18 November 2026.

This is a demonstration deployment. The policy describes how the platform is designed to handle data and should be reviewed against India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules before any real launch.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, password (stored only as a salted bcrypt hash), institution or organisation, programme, year of study and graduation year.
  • Verification material: student ID or organisation documents, and a record of automated checks and decisions.
  • Profile and documents: skills, preferences, links, your CV and an optional video resume.
  • Activity: saved opportunities, application tracker entries, grievances, notifications, and security logs (sign-ins, IP address of key actions).

Why we use it

To verify eligibility, operate discovery and tracking features, share your application with organisations you choose, prevent fraud, handle grievances, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Your CV, video resume and profile are visible only to you, HORIZON administrators, and verified organisations you apply to on HORIZON.
  • Verification documents are visible only to you and the HORIZON verification team.
  • In a grievance against an organisation, the organisation sees the grievance details; you may choose to hide your identity from them.
  • The public transparency report contains only aggregated, anonymised figures.

How we protect it

Private files are stored outside the public web root and served only after an authorisation check. Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies; requests are protected against cross-site request forgery; inputs are validated on the server; and sign-in and registration are rate-limited and protected by CAPTCHA.

Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your profile at any time, replace or remove your CV and video, and ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your data. Use the grievance desk (category “Privacy or personal data concern”) — we acknowledge within 24 hours.

Retention

Applications submitted on HORIZON are retained so both sides keep an accurate record. Replaced CVs and videos are deleted unless an application still references them. Security logs are kept for a limited period for fraud prevention.

Cookies

HORIZON uses one essential session cookie. Cloudflare Turnstile may set cookies needed to distinguish people from bots. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

Contact

Grievance Officer: see the grievance redressal page for current contact details.
